# Pipeline audit round 5 (walk-forward + exploration code) — 2026-07-02

## D1 [MAJOR] (monthly-cells) Walk-forward rank cells contain score-month prices at the month boundary, contradicting prereg v1.4's declared no-leak property

**Mechanism:** accm attributes each entry to its ENTRY month but prices the exit at to+hms (mlscreen2.py:356, 373-377) with no boundary purge. An entry in the last 24h of the last ranking month (e.g. Mar 31 for the Oct-Mar->Apr fold) has its 24h exit bar inside the score month, and that dollar PnL is summed into the ranking cell. cmd_walkforward then ranks by sum(m_pnl[rank_months]) at 24h (mlscreen2.py:552-554), so roster selection uses the score month's first-24h price path — information unavailable at deployment time (at score-month start, yesterday's 24h markouts are not yet realized). PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:141-142 declares this attribution 'forward-flowing only and cannot leak a score month into its own ranking window' — that claim is false as coded: exits crossing the boundary flow score-month prices backward into that fold's own ranking.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:356 (eout = _next_bar_close_vec(lk, to + hms)), :372-377 (accm accrual keyed on entry month over the raw ok mask), :552 (rank = sum(r["m_pnl"][i][i24] for i in rank_months)); /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/audit/edge3/PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:141-142
**Effect:** The upcoming registered walk-forward is not deployment-faithful and has a positive-bias channel: wallets whose late-rank-month entries marked out well in the score month's opening day get ranked up, and if those wallets' score-month entries share direction/exposure, roster score-month bps correlates with the selection signal. Magnitude is bounded (entries in the final 24h of a ~183-day rank window), but $-weighted ranking means one large boundary entry can flip top-50 membership near the cutoff, and the declared pass bar (pooled z > 1.64) is close to the z-magnitudes being observed (~+1), so even a small inflation matters. Fix: purge entries with to + hms >= month_end from the entry month's rank use, or lag the rank window by one day.

## D3 [MAJOR] (walkforward-cmd) Placebo z is miscalibrated when the top-50's score-month active count differs from random rosters' (activity persistence confound)

**Mechanism:** el includes wallets with zero score-month entries; stat_of (mlscreen2.py:556-558) masks them (n>=1), so the roster statistic is a mean over only the ACTIVE subset, whose size na is random. The null sd of a mean of na wallets scales ~1/sqrt(na). z = (stat - plc.mean())/plc.std() (:565) uses the placebo spread, which reflects the TYPICAL active count of random 50-draws. If top-50-by-trailing-PnL wallets are systematically more or less likely to trade in the score month than random eligible wallets (activity persistence — winners keep trading, or conversely wallets ranked top on early-window PnL are dead by the score month), the roster stat's true null sd differs from plc.std(): fewer active roster wallets inflates |z| per fold, and with the one-sided pooled-z>1.64 reading across 5 positively-dependent folds this raises the false-positive rate. Under a pure no-skill null there is no LEVEL bias (the n>=1 mask is applied identically to roster and placebos), but level bias appears if score-month activity correlates with score-month bps conditionally on trailing rank (e.g. blown-up wallets stop trading) — neither direction is controlled or even measured.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:556-567 — stat_of drops n_s=0 wallets; the code prints `active {active}/50` per fold but nothing compares it to the placebo draws' active-count distribution or rescales z. Prereg v1.4 (PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:138-140) declares 'same-size' placebos by draw count, not by active count, so the code is prereg-faithful — this is a locked design confound to be flagged for interpretation, not a code deviation.
**Effect:** The declared decision statistic (pooled z > 1.64, >=4/5 folds above placebo median) can pass or fail due to differential activity rather than markout skill. Interpretation guard for the registered run: report roster active/50 vs placebo active-count distribution per fold; if they diverge, condition placebo draws on matching active count (or restrict el to score-month-active wallets symmetrically) as a declared sensitivity, and note the caveat in the writeup before reading the result as confirmation.

## D4 [MINOR] (walkforward-cmd) Rank window consumes up to 24h of score-month prices, contradicting v1.4's 'cannot leak' declaration; Jun fold asymmetrically truncated

**Mechanism:** Per-month cells assign entries to their ENTRY month (mlscreen2.py:372-377), and a 24h-horizon entry in the final ranking month's last day has its exit priced inside the score month (eout at to+24h, :356). So the trailing-PnL ranking for each fold is partly a function of the score month's first ~24h of prices, while the score statistic is a function of score-month prices from day 1 onward — shared price data creates positive rank/score dependence under the null via short-horizon momentum/autocorrelation. Prereg v1.4 asserts this 'is forward-flowing only and cannot leak a score month into its own ranking window' (PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:142-143), which is wrong as stated: it does leak, it is just bounded at 24h. Separately, score entries in the last <24h of Jun 2026 get NaN exits (bars end at N_DAYS=334; _next_bar_close_vec :168-176 returns NaN when j >= closes.size) and are silently excluded from fold 4's score cell, whereas Feb-May folds count their month-end entries via next-month bars — a small cross-fold coverage asymmetry.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:355-377 (eout = entry ts + hms priced wherever it lands; month index from ENTRY only) and :168-176; PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:142-143.
**Effect:** Slight upward bias to per-fold percentiles (rank and score both load on the score month's opening prices) and a mildly thinner Jun score cell; both are second-order (~1 day out of ~30) but the prereg's rationale should be corrected in a Deviations note rather than left claiming zero leakage, since the walkforward's pass/fail will be quoted against that text.

## D6 [CRITICAL] (winbreadth-audition) AUC=0.81 for win_coins/win_months vs persistent/fader is ~0.78-0.80 tautological under a zero-persistence null (label leakage)

**Mechanism:** The features were computed over the FULL train window (winbreadth_audition.py:71-76 accumulates mon[] over all months; effbets_filter.py:71-72 splits halves at HALF_MS = T0+121d = Nov 30, effbets_filter.py:18). Because cand2 data starts Oct 1, the 'halves' are actually Oct-Nov (2 mo) vs Dec-Mar (4 mo), so 4 of the ~6 feature months ARE the label window. Under an iid-noise null (wallet monthly pnl has zero persistence), the class label sign(half-2 sum) correlates with each half-2 month's win indicator at bivariate-normal rho=0.5, giving P(win|persistent)=2/3 vs 1/3 per month: expected full-window win_months separates by ~1.33 months against within-class sd ~1.15 (half-1 contributes only noise common to both classes, since both are conditioned half-1-positive). That yields null AUC = Phi(1.33/(sqrt(2)*1.15)) ~ 0.79. win_coins leaks identically through half-2 coin-month pnl (cm dict, winbreadth_audition.py:75-76).
**Evidence:** winbreadth_audition.py:71-76 (full-window feature accumulation), effbets_filter.py:18,71-72 (half definition; asymmetric 2mo/4mo halves given Oct 1 data start). The audition's own CAUSAL test of the same construct — signal C, rank-window-only win-breadth (winbreadth_audition.py:92-102,116) — scored z=+0.49, consistent with the marginal real signal being ~0.01-0.03 AUC above the ~0.79 tautological floor.
**Effect:** The claim 'win_coins/win_months separates persistent from fader wallets at AUC 0.81' is arithmetic, not evidence: any noise process produces ~0.79 with these definitions. Any research decision (e.g. adding win-breadth floors or ranks to the registered walk-forward) motivated by the 0.81 is unsupported; the causal audition z=+0.49 is the number that should govern, and it says near-nothing forward-looking.

## D7 [MAJOR] (winbreadth-audition) Signal B's z is not comparable to A/C's z — the floors reshape the placebo field itself, hiding any field-level effect of the floors

**Mechanism:** For signal B, wallets failing win_coins>=3 & win_months>=2 are dropped BEFORE el is built (winbreadth_audition.py:114-115), and the 1000 placebos are drawn from that same floored el (line 123). **Evidence:** winbreadth_audition.py:114-115 (field-level exclusion), 116-118 (B still ranks by pnl), 122-126 (roster and placebos both from the signal-specific el).

The comparison as run cannot reject 'floors add value'; only the A-vs-C contrast (same field, same placebos) is internally comparable.

## D12 [MAJOR] (effbets-and-halves) Position reconstruction is anchored at the Oct 1 carry-in, so 'flat' anchors mark return-to-unknown-baseline and can sign-flip every downstream markout

**Mechanism:** cum = np.cumsum(sz) (effbets_filter.py:38) starts from the first available row, which -- given the missing Aug/Sep cand2 months -- is Oct 1, not the wallet's true position history from T0 (line 25 filters ts >= T0 = Aug 1, implying full-window reconstruction was intended). For a wallet carrying a position P on Oct 1, cum measures position-minus-P, so the leading-flat anchor flat = |cum|*px < 1 (line 39) fires when the wallet RETURNS TO P, not when it is flat. A fixed-clip trader (carry +2 BTC, closes to zero, reopens +2 BTC) anchors at a moment it is actually long 2 BTC; every subsequent sgn_c, comp, and open/close classification (lines 47-57) is offset by -P, so closes are scored as opposite-direction opens with sign-flipped 24h markouts.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/effbets_filter.py:25, :38-43, :47-57; cand2_202508/202509 absent from scratch_conv/mlscreen/; mlscreen2's audited markout paths only run behind _require_complete, which this script skips.
**Effect:** For any wallet with a nonzero Oct 1 carry-in, ep_pnl, day_pnl, tot, and especially h1 (the early window, where the corrupted anchors concentrate) contain wrong-signed and wrong-sized entries. This contaminates both the eff_bets rankings and the h1-vs-h2 persistence numbers, in a way that is invisible because the script silently proceeded on partial months.

## D13 [MAJOR] (effbets-and-halves) day_pnl cells are keyed by entry day but realized over t..t+24h, so adjacent coin-day cells share price paths and eff_day overstates independent profit days

**Mechanism:** day_pnl keys on to[ok] // 86_400_000 (effbets_filter.py:68-69) while pnl for each entry is so*(e24-ein) with e24 taken at t+24h (lines 59, 63). An entry at 23:00 on day D is assigned to cell (coin, D) but nearly all of its exposure is day D+1's price path, which it shares with every day-D+1 entry. Adjacent-day |pnl| cells are therefore positively dependent, and the HHI-based eff_day (lines 78-80) counts one 24-48h price move straddling midnight as two or more independent 'bet days'.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/effbets_filter.py:59, :63, :68-69, :78-80.
**Effect:** eff_day is inflated most for wallets that trade continuously through day boundaries -- precisely the wallets a day-concentration screen is meant to catch -- so the accept/reject evaluation of the filter using eff_day understates concentration and can pass wallets whose PnL comes from a single multi-hour event. Cells should be non-overlapping (e.g., key by entry day with markout truncated at day end, or use 2-day buckets) before eff_day is used in a registered decision.

## D14 [MINOR] (effbets-and-halves) Dust misalignment at the anchor: flat flags come from pre-drop cum while comp/signs use recomputed post-drop cum, and dust-scale entries get full weight in the unweighted bps sums

**Mechanism:** flat is computed on the pre-drop cumsum (effbets_filter.py:39) and sliced as fl = flat[ff+1:] (line 44), but cum is re-cumsummed after the drop (line 47), offsetting every subsequent position by the anchor dust cum_pre[ff] (up to $1 notional by the flat threshold). So episode boundaries (from fl) and comp/sgn_c (from the shifted cum) can disagree wherever positions are within dust of flat, and shifted dust positions can still pass comp > 1e-12 & crossed (line 53), emitting entries whose bps observations enter h1/h2/tot with the same unit weight as large entries (lines 64, 70-72).
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/effbets_filter.py:39, :44, :47, :53, :64, :70-72.
**Effect:** Bounded by the $1 flat threshold, so ep_pnl/day_pnl dollar aggregates are essentially unaffected; the exposure is to the equal-weighted bps statistics (h1_bps/h2_bps/bps_sum) for wallets whose positions hover near the dust boundary, where spurious full-weight bps observations can be created. Recomputing flat from the post-drop cum (or from |cum - cum_pre[ff]| pre-slice) removes the inconsistency.

## D17 [MAJOR] (decision-integrity) AUC=0.81 for win_coins/win_months separating 'persistent' from 'fader' wallets is circular: features span the full window that also defines the class labels

**Mechanism:** The persistent/fader labels are half-window outcomes (effbets_filter.py stores h1_bps/h1_n/h2_bps/h2_n per wallet, lines 81-83: persistent = positive in both halves, fader = positive h1, negative h2). The win_coins/win_months features were computed over the FULL train window (winbreadth build writes per-month pnl for every train month, winbreadth_audition.py:71-76; win_months = count of positive months, win_coins from cm across all months). A wallet that is positive in the second half mechanically accrues more winning months and more positive coin-months in exactly the months that define its label — the feature contains the label. Under a pure-noise null with no persistence at all, full-window breadth still separates the classes. So AUC=0.81 is an upper bound heavily inflated by leakage, and it is the number that motivated signal B's specific thresholds (win_coins>=3, win_months>=2, hardcoded at winbreadth_audition.py:114), which were then 'validated' on audition folds whose rank windows overlap the window the thresholds were fit on.
**Evidence:** effbets_filter.py:71-72,81-83 (half-window label ingredients); winbreadth_audition.py:70-80 (full-window monthly cells), :92-102 (features), :114 (thresholds wc<3 or wm<2 as the graduated filter).
**Effect:** The belief 'breadth features predict persistence (AUC 0.81)' should be discarded as evidence; the honest version computes features on the first half only and labels on the second half only. Signal B's threshold values are partially in-sample tuned; its 3/3 audition record (finding 5) cannot launder that. ## D18 [MAJOR] (decision-integrity) The audition's accept/reject calls (reject win-breadth at z=+0.49, graduate win floors at 3/3, call baseline 'encouraging' at pooled z=+1.18) exceed what 3 dependent folds x 3 signals can support

**Mechanism:** Pooled z is the arithmetic mean of 3 per-fold z's (winbreadth_audition.py:130). Under independence its null sd is 0.58; the folds' rank windows overlap 2 of 3 months (line 89) so rosters and fold z's are positively correlated and the true sd is higher — meaning +1.18 is under 2 sigma and +0.49 vs +1.18 is a difference of ~1 sigma or less, on rosters that themselves overlap across signals (A and C rank the same wallets by correlated keys). Rejecting signal C on that gap is a coin-flip decision. The 3/3-folds-above-median criterion used to graduate signal B has null probability 1/8 per signal; across 3 signals tried on the SAME folds, P(at least one shows 3/3) is about 1-(7/8)^3 = 33% even before positive dependence raises it. Note also B graduated despite a LOWER pooled z (+0.96) than the baseline A (+1.18) — the graduation criterion (fold pattern) differs from the rejection criterion (pooled z), i.e. the winning statistic was chosen per signal, post hoc. Per-fold statistics are additionally heavy-tailed: any roster wallet with a single score-month entry (n>=1, line 120) contributes its one-fill bps at full equal weight, so fold z's are noisy and 3 of them cannot resolve sub-1-sigma signal differences.
**Evidence:** winbreadth_audition.py:89 (overlapping folds), :104 (3 signals, same folds, shared rng stream), :111 (n<15 ad-hoc eligibility), :119-121 (n>=1 single-entry wallets at full weight), :126 (per-fold z), :130 (pooled = mean of z's); claims context: A +1.18 / B +0.96 / C +0.49, B graduated on 3/3.
**Effect:** Decisions (3), (4), (5) are all under-powered: C's rejection and B's graduation are not statistically distinguishable from A, and none clears a multiplicity-adjusted bar. Concretely: treat the audition as hypothesis-generating only; carry ALL of A/B/C (or a pre-declared subset) into a single registered comparison with a Bonferroni-adjusted bar on the untouched folds (per finding 1), rather than letting today's z ordering silently pre-select the tournament field.
