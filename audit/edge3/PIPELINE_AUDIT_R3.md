## B2 [MAJOR] (bars-ingestion) Train-placed straddling entries land in the pre-registered TEST cell: te = ok & ~tr assigns any entry whose exit crosses the split to test_pnl

**Mechanism:** cmd_markout classifies per horizon: tr = ok & (to + hms < day_split_ms); te = ok & ~tr (mlscreen2.py:321-322). The prereg (v1.2 item 1) declares only the train side of this convention ('train cell only if EXIT lands before split'); the code's complement dumps entries OPENED inside the train window (to in [split-h, split)) into the TEST statistic that cmd_look_markout scores (te[top].sum(), :432-437). For the 24h primary that is all Mar-31 entries; for the 168h secondary a full week of Mar 25-31 entries (~8% of the 91-day test mass at that horizon). These entries are placed under the train regime by wallets ranked on train_pnl at the same horizon, and their markout windows overlap the very late-March price paths that produced the ranking PnL (an entry Mar 20 exit Mar 27 counted in train shares its path with an entry Mar 26 exit Apr 2 counted in test), so the leak is selection-correlated, not symmetric noise. The treatment is also asymmetric at the data edge: test entries with exits past Jun 30 are dropped (ok=False), but train entries with exits past Mar 31 are added to test.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:321-322 (tr/te split), :432-441 (test statistic and placebo built from test_pnl/test_n), :264 (168h in declared ladder); PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md v1.2 items 1-2 ('roster's TEST-window markout PnL' vs code putting train-window entries in it).
**Effect:** The decision statistic of the co-primary family is contaminated with train-window, selection-correlated PnL — biased toward PASS via winners'-continuation, worst for the 168h cell (a week of train entries) and present in the 24h primary (one day). Placebo rosters get their own straddlers so the null shifts too, but the correlation with the train ranking is what the roster has and random rosters do not. Fix before the look: te should require to >= day_split_ms (entry in test window), with straddlers excluded from both cells.

## B3 [MINOR] (bars-ingestion) 5m bar close is an arbitrary pick among same-block prints: sort_by('ts').last() has no tiebreaker and is not order-stable under the streaming engine, making bars — and therefore every pre-registered number — non-reproducible

**Mechanism:** cmd_bars computes close=pl.col('px').sort_by('ts').last() inside a streaming group_by (mlscreen.py:55-57). ts is the HL block timestamp: every fill in the bar's final block shares one ms, and an aggressive order sweeping the book emits multiple same-ts fills at different price levels. sort_by keys on ts alone and, under the multithreaded/streaming engine, row order within ties is not guaranteed, so 'last' among same-ms prints is arbitrary — the close of any bar whose last block contained >1 print in the coin can land anywhere across that sweep's levels, and can differ run to run. The fill-level tid (kept in fills.parquet by fills_to_parquet.py:37) would break ties deterministically but is unused. Every downstream price is built on these closes: markout ein/eout via _next_bar_close_vec, follower fills, daily MTM closes.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen.py:55-57 (sort_by ts only, streaming sink); /Users/corywagamaneure/projects/ml-forecasting-research/experiments/address_profile/fills_to_parquet.py:37 (tid available, unused as tiebreaker); /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:137-145,204 (all pricing consumes these closes).
**Effect:** Adds arbitrary intra-block noise (up to the sweep depth, largest in the thin alts where top-50 PnL concentrates) to every markout entry and exit — roughly zero-mean for the point estimates already shown, but it breaks the single-look guarantee operationally: regenerating bars (which MUST happen for 202508/202509/202606) can change closes for already-covered months' bars only if those files are rebuilt, and any rebuild silently changes ein/eout, the top-50 roster, and the test statistic with no way to reconcile against the inspected numbers. Pin determinism before the look (sort_by(['ts','tid']) or take px of max-(ts,tid) row) and never regenerate existing month bars.

## B4 [MAJOR] (markout-stage) Reversal (sign-flip) fills mis-attributed: full fill size counted as 'opening', and flips that shrink |position| are silently dropped

**Mechanism:** cmd_markout defines opening = (|cum| > |pos_before|) & crossed and then uses the FULL fill size so = sz[oi] for pnl = so*(eout-ein) and notional = |so*ein| (mlscreen2.py:300-305, 316-318). For a long->short flip (pos_before=+10, sz=-25, cum=-15) the true newly-opened short is |cum|=15 units, but 25 units of markout PnL and notional are booked — the 10 closing units' post-fill price move is imported into 'entry markout' with the NEW direction's sign, amplifying measured edge for wallets that flip at turning points. Conversely a flip where the new position is smaller than the old (+10 -> -3) fails |cum|>|pos_before| and is not counted as an entry at all, even though 3 units opened. The identical logic is duplicated in mlscreen_reports.py cmd_extract50 (lines 81-96), so the per-entry characterization dataset carries the same distortion.
**Evidence:** mlscreen2.py:300-305 (opening = (np.abs(cum) > np.abs(pos_before)) & crossed; to, so = t[oi], sz[oi]), :316-318 (pnl = so*(eout-ein); notl = np.abs(so*ein)); mlscreen_reports.py:81-96 same construction; prereg v1.2 item 1 says 'every position-INCREASING taker fill' with no partial-attribution rule, so the convention is unpinned.
**Effect:** Distorts both sides shown/upcoming: (a) train-side — top-50 roster membership is chosen by dollar train markout PnL, which is inflated for flip-heavy wallets, so the shown +75bp@24h/+193bp@168h describes a roster partly selected on close-out PnL misbooked as entry edge, and the 87%-majors / crowding / direction characterization inherits it; (b) the pre-registered look's test statistic is the same dollar PnL, so the 24h co-primary cell measures a quantity that is not per-entry markout for any wallet that reverses positions, over-weighting exactly the wallets the ranking favored.

## B6 [MINOR] (markout-stage) Train-window entries with post-split exits are reassigned into the TEST cells — boundary contamination with no purge, and a prereg semantics mismatch

## B10 [MAJOR] (reports-characterization) Timestamp-tie nondeterminism: unstable sort('ts') means the extract50 entry population and the markout ranking itself are not pinned functions of the data

## B13 [CRITICAL] (prereg-look-fidelity) Markout co-primary placebo is not notional-matched: dollar-sum test statistic makes the p97.5 pass bar a near-coin-flip under a zero-skill null (fatal calibration flaw of the declared cell)

**Mechanism:** cmd_look_markout ranks by TRAIN dollar markout PnL (tr = train_pnl, top = argsort(-tr)[:50]) and scores stat = te[top].sum() against plc = te[rng.choice(eligible,50)].sum() over 1000 draws (mlscreen2.py:431-439). Train dollar PnL is the product of notional and bps, so the top-50-by-$ roster is mechanically the largest-notional wallets in the field (a wallet cannot post +$1M train markout on $50k of flow; the eligible field spans $500 mean-notional retail to whales with no upper bound). Random 50-wallet placebo rosters have aggregate test notional orders of magnitude smaller than the roster's. Under a pure zero-skill null (every wallet's test markout zero-mean), stat ~ N(0, sigma_roster^2) while the placebo p97.5 threshold is ~2*sigma_placebo with sigma_placebo << sigma_roster; P(stat > p97.5 | null) then approaches 50%, not the declared 2.5%. Under the field's actual negative drift (-16bp@24h) the giant roster notional cuts the other way, so the test can also spuriously FAIL a genuinely informed roster. Either way the placebo is not exchangeable with the roster in the statistic's dominant driver (notional), so 'beats 1,000 random same-size rosters' carries almost no evidence about skill — it mostly measures whether the biggest wallets' Q2-2026 flow happened to be net profitable, on top of winner's-curse reversion from selecting on size-times-luck in train. The flaw is in prereg v1.2 item 2 itself ('random same-size rosters' = same wallet COUNT, not matched notional), so it is fixable pre-look by amendment: (i) notional-matched placebos (draw rosters stratified to match the roster's train-24h-notional decile composition), or (ii) make the decision statistic per-notional (roster test bps = sum(test_pnl)/sum(test_notional), placebo identical) — noting the eq-wt variant is currently declared 'diagnostic only' so promoting it must be recorded as a pre-look deviation. The team already solved this exact problem in the follower cell (cmd_analyze weights every wallet 1/train-gross, mlscreen2.py:384, so its placebo IS scale-fair) — the markout cell simply lacks the analogous normalization.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:431-439 (dollar-sum stat and unmatched placebo) vs :384 (follower cell's 1/gross normalization); /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/audit/edge3/PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md:89-92 ('roster = top-50 by TRAIN dollar markout PnL at 24h ... vs 1,000 random same-size rosters — pass bar p97.5'); context: field -16bp@24h dollar-weighted drift already shown
**Effect:** The upcoming pre-registered markout look is miscalibrated before it runs: PASS at 24h would not distinguish informed flow from 'whales had a lucky quarter' (~40-50% false-pass rate under the null instead of 2.5%), and a true edge can spuriously fail on drift-times-notional. If not amended and recorded in the Deviations section BEFORE the look, the co-primary family's verdict is uninterpretable in either direction.
