## F2 [CRITICAL] The '15-min lag' is hourly-candle-quantized: entries are priced at hourly closes whose effective timestamp averages 15-30 min BEFORE the leader's fill, crediting pre-fill run-up + leader impact; the lag sweep's own decay implies the honestly post-fill-priced subset has ~zero-to-negative edge, and the reworked GO gate's selection leg inherits the same estimator (voiding the round-1 'gate reads realized fills' immunity)

- **Dimension(s):** selci-method,data-pricing  |  **Confidence:** 0.85  |  **Verifier kills:** 0/3
- **Anchor:** scripts/selci_fh.py

**Claim attacked:** 'lag-robust ... priced at a 15-min follower lag ... +24-31bp net of realistic cost' (audit/EDGE_INVESTIGATION.md Results 3-4; the deployed operating point) AND the deployed gate's 'selection eof CI_low >= 8bp' leg, which uses the same pricing

**Failure scenario:** selci_fh.cmd_extract prices entry as _close_at(lookup, et+lag) on HOURLY candles; _close_at returns the last candle FULLY CLOSED by t, i.e. a price whose true timestamp is uniform in (t-60min, t], mean t-30min. Effective entry-pricing time per sweep cell: lag=60s -> mean et-29.5min; 5m -> et-25min; 15m -> et-15min; 30m -> et+0min. The 'survives 15-min lag' number therefore marks entry on average 15 minutes BEFORE the copied wallet even traded, crediting the follower with pre-entry momentum plus the wallet's own taker impact. The sweep's own decay (eof 71->64->37->21 as effective pricing time moves toward et) proves ~50bp of trade-direction move happens in the ~30min INTO entry and is harvested by the estimator, not by any real follower; solving the pre/post-fill mixture (measured(L)=p_pre*E_pre+p_post*E_post, E_pre≈71) gives E_post ≈ −65bp at 15m lag — the +24-31bp headline is carried entirely by the pre-fill-stale-priced majority. A live follower's effective pricing time >= et maps to the 30-min column (+21 gross @1h) or beyond, and the doc itself says 'Only the 30-min-lag corner breaks' at conservative cost — the net headline plausibly goes to roughly -5..+10bp. This is the incomplete round-1 fix: SUMMARY.md item 5 ordered a sub-candle re-run (03-F1/F4); selci_fh reused the same hourly candles. The 'realized-net' profitability GO leg is not realized PnL — it is a beta-hedged SYNTHETIC. gate_feed.py:318 feeds decide() `neutralize_top(realized, basket, beta=config.beta)`, i.e. raw_bps minus dir*1.245*basket_ret per harvest, and experiment.py:389 gates GO on that series' CI (verdict text even calls it 'realized net-of-cost'). The deployed executor holds NO basket hedge (harvest_runner.py trades only the tranche legs; equity() at line 92-99 marks the unhedged book), and the hedge is priced free — no spread, fees, impact, borrow or funding on a 190-coin alt-basket short. The realized maxDD bound uses the same synthetic curve (assemble_results line 269).

**Why it matters:** Concrete failure: a 14-day gate window where the alt basket falls ~8% and roster opens are (typically) long-tilted — actual paper equity bleeds hundreds of bps, but each long harvest gets +1.245*|basket drop| ADDED back, so realized_net CI_low>0 and the gate certifies 'profitable' on a book that lost money; symmetrically a genuinely profitable rally fortnight can be forced to NO_GO. The GO answers 'is the beta-hedged construct positive at a frozen beta' — a question no deployable book (which cannot short this basket at zero cost) can cash. None of the 18 confirmed findings (funding, fast-lag, block bootstrap, power, maxDD-compounding) touches the neutralization/hedge mismatch.

## G2. Frozen beta=1.245 turns BOTH gate legs and live roster selection into a market-direction regime bet, and no swarm dimension traced it past the offline estimate. beta appears only as hardcoded defaults (main.py:75 locked_config, selci_fh.py:575 etc. — no derivation anywhere in-repo, round-1 Audit 04 noted this for the OFFLINE number only). Live: selection ranks trailing-30d trimmed-mean of markouts neutralized at 1.245 (roll via SelectionAdapter), the gate's eof leg neutralizes both arms at 1.245 (gate_feed.py:105-106,143-145), and the realized leg subtracts 1.245*basket (gate_feed.py:318). If the live-regime beta differs, the train-window residual d*(beta_true-1.245)*basket_train systematically favors wallets tilted in the trend direction, so the committed roster carries a directional tilt vs the field; the forward eof point estimate then contains tilt*(beta_true-1.245)*basket_forward.

**Why it matters:** The measurement averaged this over 4 folds spanning trend reversals; the deployed gate is ONE ~14-day window. A single trending fortnight with beta mis-specified by ~0.2-0.4 on alts (equal-weight 190-coin basket, cross-regime) can contribute more than the 8bp MAR to eof with zero selection skill — clearing the selection leg — while the same term inflates/deflates the realized leg identically (finding #1). The coin-comovement finding covers CI clustering, not this systematic directional term in the POINT estimates of both GO conditions; Audit 04's known status makes it look 'already handled' when its gate-inheritance was never audited.

## G3. The validated 'rolling past-only top-1500 universe' has NO live implementation — the deployed candidate pool is a frozen, builder-less CSV that ages without bound. main.py:274 defaults markout mode to data/follow/markout_candidates.csv (2431 wallets); grep shows no builder anywhere in the repo (only consumers), and per other_repo_notes/README.txt those wallets descend from the third-party export's superset ranked by Feb–May 2026 cumulative TAKER notional. Every 14-day reroll (live.py:203-213 → scheduler.roll) re-ranks the SAME frozen cohort; there is no mechanism (no full-population tape live) to re-derive the top-N-by-past-notional universe the study declared essential — the exact property whose absence produced the discredited +159bp (round-1 H2/01). The 'fix' for H2 swapped one unverifiable frozen CSV (long_hold_wallets) for another (markout_candidates), with the identical 'no in-repo builder' defect.

**Why it matters:** Two mechanisms: (a) staleness — by the second/third reroll the roster AND the gate's field control (gate_feed.py:124-152 filters the same frozen candidates) are drawn from a cohort selected on 2-5-month-old activity; informed alt wallets churn (the repo's own copy-trade study found HFT/alpha wallet rotation), so the live edge decays relative to the fold-measured one where the eligible pool refreshed monthly; the gate scored at day ~14 certifies a system whose defining input degrades monotonically afterward, and the confirmed no-stopping-rule finding means it keeps trading on that certification indefinitely. (b) The gate's 'follow everyone' null is a within-frozen-cohort null, not the study's null — new post-May wallets can never enter either arm, so eof live is a different estimand than the validated +24-31bp.

## G4. Single-source, unverifiable data provenance plus an unreconciled conviction-filter reimplementation. The ENTIRE measurement chain — fills_his per-wallet parquets, past_univ.json, superset_wallets.csv, the zhash TWAP/liquidation flag, and the taker-notional universe ranking — is one export from a different repo/author (other_repo_notes/README.txt) with no checksums, no construction code, and reference numbers this repo cannot reproduce; split_his_fills.py merely reshapes it (and hardcodes startPosition=0.0 for every fill). Meanwhile the LIVE conviction filter is an independent implementation: offline conviction = his boolean zhash; live conviction = API `hash` != ZERO_HASH with a FAIL-OPEN default — watcher.py:159 `f.get("hash", "")` and fills_source.py:51 `str(f.get("hash", ""))` both map a missing hash to conviction=True. No test reconciles his zhash semantics (which bundle TWAP AND liquidation) against the live API hash field.

**Why it matters:** Mechanism 1 (measurement): if his export's zhash over/under-flags (e.g., liquidation fills carry real hashes on-chain, or his TWAP detection differs from the API's zero-hash convention), the scored 'conviction open' population offline is not the population live harvests — the +24-31bp was measured on a set the executor cannot reproduce, and the divergence is invisible because both sides look internally consistent. Mechanism 2 (live): any API response variant that omits/renames hash silently classifies ALL fills as conviction, flooding the harvest with mechanical TWAP slices the study explicitly excluded as non-informative — diluting the live edge toward zero while the selection leg (built from the same fail-open RestFillsProvider rows) degrades in tandem, so nothing alarms. A one-line schema drift by the exchange changes the deployed strategy with zero error surfaced.

## G5. Coin-listing survivorship in the measurement, and its live mirror. The study prices only coins present in data/follow/candles (191 files == the 190-coin alt_universe.txt), fetched from the live HL API (candles_source.py fetch_lookups: a coin that errors/returns nothing 'is skipped — it just won't price'); candleSnapshot only serves currently-listed perps, so any perp DELISTED during Feb–Jun 2026 has no candle file and every open in it is silently dropped from both train skill and test markouts (selci_fh.py cmd_extract: `if c not in coins: continue`; same filter in convergence_his.py reshape_month). Live, the same frozen 190-coin list means post-deploy listings are never harvestable, and a coin delisted mid-hold leaves its tranche permanently un-exitable (harvest_runner.py:166 stale_book_exit retries forever) — never entering the realized journal.

**Why it matters:** Delist-bound alts are the extreme left tail (delisting follows volume/price collapse): wallets' positions in them — often the selected 'skilled' wallets' worst catastrophic holds — are censored from the fixed-horizon markouts, biasing both the absolute +30bp log-growth expectation and (to the extent selected and field wallets differ in delist-coin exposure) the eof itself upward. Live, the failure is asymmetric and gate-invisible: a delisting during a 6h hold produces a real total-loss-class outcome that the realized-net leg structurally cannot record (the tranche just never realizes), so the profitability CI is conditioned on 'coin survived the hold' — a survivorship filter applied to the exact tail events that would break the GO.
