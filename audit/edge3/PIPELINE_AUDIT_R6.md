## E1 [CRITICAL] (exmajors-artifact) Signal F's rank cells have the exact D1 month-boundary leak that was declared critical and fixed in mlscreen2 — venue_split never got the fix

**Mechanism:** venue_split.py bins each entry into its ENTRY month with no requirement that the 24h exit lands in the same month: line 56-58 does `mi = month_idx(int(to[j])); if 0 <= mi < 11: tgt[mi] += [so[j]*(e24[j]-ein[j]), 1, ...]`. So every taker entry in the last 24h of the final rank month is marked at the SCORE month's opening prices (e24 = first bar close after t+24h). The fold then ranks wallets by `rank_cells[rank_m, 0].sum()` (line 84-87) — a dollar-PnL sum that has read the score month's first-day prices. The only exit guard in the file is `t + H24 < SPLIT_MS` (line 48), which protects the Apr-1 train/test split, not month boundaries. Prereg amendment v1.5 item 1 (D1/D4) explicitly calls this pattern a falsified 'cannot leak' claim — 'boundary-crossing exits read the score month's opening prices into the ranking' — and the fix was applied to mlscreen2.py's accm cells (line 373-379: entry counts only if `_cmonth_idx(int(to[j]) + hms) == mi`). venue_split.py, written after that fix, omits it.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/venue_split.py:48,55-58 (no exit-month check) vs /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/mlscreen2.py:372-379 (D1 fix: `if 0 <= mi < 11 and _cmonth_idx(int(to[j]) + hms) == mi`); prereg /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/audit/edge3/PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md v1.5 item 1.
**Effect:** Ranking mechanically favors wallets whose alt positions gapped up into the score month's first day. In momentum-trending microcaps (exactly the ex-majors slice), a coin rallying across the boundary both boosts the wallet's leaked rank PnL and its early score-month entry markouts — a selection-to-score coupling that 800 random placebo rosters cannot share, inflating every fold z. This directly attacks the +1.81 standout: the leak is per-month, so it operates in all 3 folds, and the Jan fold ('the fold no other signal survived') ranks on Oct-Dec where Dec 31 entries read Jan 1 prices. The z=+1.81 / 3-for-3 result is unreliable until venue_split is rerun with the mlscreen2.py:379 exit-in-month condition.

## E2 [MAJOR] (exmajors-artifact) No winsorization: raw unclipped bps with n>=1 per-wallet means lets a single microcap entry dominate a fold, and ranking by trailing alt $PnL selects exactly the wallets carrying such tails

**Mechanism:** venue_split.py line 59 accumulates raw `np.sign(so[j]) * (e24[j]/ein[j] - 1) * 1e4` — no clipping — into month cells, while the sibling pipeline crowdrel.py line 47 clips the identical quantity to ±500bp. The fold statistic (lines 89-91) is `mean([b/n for _,n,b in items if n>=1])` over the top-50: a wallet with a single (n=1) score-month alt entry counts with weight equal to a 500-entry wallet. Critically, the coupling is selective: ranking by trailing ex-majors dollar markout PnL (col 0, line 87-88) preferentially picks wallets with large concentrated positions in extreme-volatility alts, so the top-50's score cells are systematically fatter-tailed than random placebo rosters'; the z formula (line 96) then treats the placebo distribution as Gaussian, so a fat-tail-driven roster mean converts into an inflated nominal z.
**Evidence:** /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/venue_split.py:59 (raw bps), :89-91 (n>=1 equal-weight wallet means), :96 (normal-theory z) vs /Users/corywagamaneure/bablyon/scratch_conv/crowdrel.py:2,47 (`np.clip(..., -500, 500)`).
**Effect:** The +1.81 pooled z may ride a handful of extreme alt entries held by selected wallets rather than repeatable skill. This also cleanly explains the day's cross-signal pattern: the winsorized crowd-relative rankings (+0.48/+0.23) underperforming the unwinsorized F is what you'd expect if the tails, not the center, carry F's statistic. Minimum re-run: recompute F's folds with the crowdrel ±500 clip and with the top-1 score entry per wallet removed; if z collapses, F is a tail artifact.

## E6 [CRITICAL] (crowdrel-and-audition) crowdrel LOO is leave-one-ENTRY-out, not leave-wallet-out: wallets crowd against their own fills, crushing excess for exactly the active wallets the screen targets

**Mechanism:** entries_of (crowdrel.py:40-48) treats every position-increasing crossed FILL as a separate entry, and cand2 is fill-level (tid granularity, mlscreen2.py:70-81), so a multi-fill order produces many near-identical bps values in the same (coin, day, dir) cell. In wallets mode the LOO mean (crowdrel.py:92, cm=(cell[0]-b_)/(cell[1]-1)) removes only the current entry; the wallet's OTHER same-cell entries stay in the 'crowd'. If a wallet holds k of a cell's n entries, its excess is attenuated by ~(n-k)/(n-1); the cell[1]>=5 gate (crowdrel.py:89) can be satisfied ENTIRELY by the wallet's own fills, making excess ~0 by construction with no true crowd present. The prereg itself records (signal-E amendment, PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md ~line 204) that fill counts inflate 10-100x for multi-fill orders and same-order fills share identical next-bar markouts. (Verified safe: division by zero at cell[1]==1 is impossible given the <5 skip; the JSON round(.,2) on cell sums is negligible.)
**Evidence:** crowdrel.py:40-48 (fill-level entries), crowdrel.py:89-92 (skip <5, then LOO over all entries incl. own), mlscreen2.py:70-81 (cand2 = raw fills per tid, no order grouping); PREREG_WALLET_SCREEN.md signal-E amendment documenting 10-100x fill inflation with identical markouts
**Effect:** Excess-mean and hit-rate-vs-crowd rankings are systematically biased toward zero for high-activity/execution-algo wallets — the same wallets that top the raw-$PnL baseline — so crowdrel's underperformance (+0.48/+0.23 vs baseline ~+1.1) is substantially a mechanical artifact of the estimator, not evidence against H6. The H6 'majors identifiability' hypothesis is being previewed with a broken instrument.

## E13 [MAJOR] (meta-methodology) The n>=1 equal-weight statistic with NO winsorization in winbreadth/venue_split makes fold z's non-standard and non-comparable across signals; ex-majors (F) is the most tail-exposed cell, and comparing it against the clipped crowdrel z's confounds clipping with signal
